ListingAI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

ListingAI (“we”, “us”) is operated by Yamshi. This policy explains how we handle data when you install and use our Shopify app.

Data we access

  • Shop domain and OAuth access token (to create/update products)
  • Product titles and content you submit for AI generation
  • Optional image URLs you provide
  • Billing status via Shopify Billing API

How we use data

We use shop credentials only to generate listings and publish products you approve. Text and images you submit may be sent to our AI provider (OpenRouter) to generate copy. We do not sell merchant data.

Storage

We store shop tokens and usage counts on our servers to operate the app and enforce free-plan limits.

Uninstall

When you uninstall, we stop accessing your store. You may request deletion of stored shop records by emailing support.
